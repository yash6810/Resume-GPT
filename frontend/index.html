Privacy & Zero-Knowledge Architecture

      

      
    

    

      

        Zero Data Selling Guarantee: ResumeGPT is open-source and privacy-first. We do not sell your personal data, resumes, or application history to recruiters, data brokers, or advertisers.
      

      
1. Data Storage & Encryption

      
All resumes, job tracker entries, and generated drafts are stored locally in your browser storage by default, or encrypted in your private database account when signed in. API transmissions are strictly encrypted via TLS 1.3.

      
2. AI & Model Usage

      
Resume parsing and ATS heuristic calculations run locally or via dedicated secure models. We do not train public AI models on your private resumes.

      
3. Self-Service Deletion (GDPR Article 17)

      
You retain 100% ownership of your data. You may export or permanently delete your account and all associated documents at any time from your Account Settings with one click.
